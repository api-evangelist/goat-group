# Goat Group

> Goat Group is the parent company behind a family of global marketplaces for sneakers, apparel, and luxury goods — the GOAT marketplace and app, the Flight Club consignment stores, the Grailed peer-to-peer menswear community, and the alias B2B platform. Founded in 2015 by Eddy Lu and Daishin Sugano and headquartered in Los Angeles (Culver City), California, it pioneered ship-to-verify authentication for resale. Goat Group does not currently publish a public developer API program; this is a network identity record.
